## Suite — 31 tests × 2 projects

Every test runs on both projects (31 × 2 = 62). The single skip is
`touch.spec.ts` on `desktop`: the spec is mobile-only by design
(describe-level skip — desktop never even boots the page fixture).

### options.spec.ts — 2 tests

- invalid values show an error and do not save
- a valid value saves and persists after page.reload()

### gameplay.spec.ts — 4 tests

- a match starts: assets load and the state becomes running
- holding thrust and turn changes position and angle within 1s
- thrusting into the border pins the ship at the arena limit
- the ship never enters an island cell

### combat.spec.ts — 6 tests

- front fire launches one projectile, the broadside launches three
- two shots inside the cooldown window produce a single projectile
- killing a placed enemy scores +1, exactly once
- a chaser exploding on the player costs hp and no score
- a shooter inside its attack range fires enemy projectiles
- one spawn interval brings an enemy onto the field

### match-flow.spec.ts — 7 tests

- the session limit ends the match by time
- a destroyed ship ends the match by death
- once ended, advancing changes nothing
- Play Again starts a clean match
- pausing freezes the clock; resuming is a click that accumulates nothing
- the result screen shows score, time and reason — and survives a reload
- leaving a running match to the menu records nothing in the history

### ranking-history.spec.ts — 8 tests

- the boards show their loading state and then the loaded data
- the boards show the empty state under the empty scenario
- the ranking error state recovers through Retry
- pagination walks to page 2 and back
- a finished match lands in both tabs
- a rejected submission resumes after a reload
- a timeout after the write resends without duplicating the record
- a late answer never overwrites the newer page

### touch.spec.ts — 1 test (skipped on desktop)

- touch pad (mobile only) › two fingers at once: thrust moves and the front
  gun fires; releasing stops the ship

### visual.spec.ts — 3 tests (local-only, excluded from CI)

- main menu
- arena at rest
- result screen after the clock runs the match out
